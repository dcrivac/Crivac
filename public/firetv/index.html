Features

Everything on one screen

Full Fire TV navigation and Bluetooth speaker control without switching apps or picking up a second remote.

📺

Complete Fire TV Control

All the buttons from your physical remote, reimagined for iPhone. Navigate, play, and explore without digging between cushions.

  • D-pad with long-press repeat for fast scrolling
  • Home, Back, Menu, Alexa mic
  • Play/Pause, Rewind, Fast-Forward
  • TV volume Up, Down, and Mute
  • Connects over your home Wi-Fi via ADB
🔊

JBL Bluetooth Volume

When your JBL Clip 4 (or any Bluetooth speaker) is connected, a live system volume slider appears — no separate controls needed.

  • Real iOS system volume slider — actually works
  • Live percentage readout
  • Shows connected Bluetooth device name
  • Automatically appears when BT audio is active
⚡

Fast & Lightweight

Built natively in Swift with zero third-party dependencies. No subscriptions, no accounts, no cloud — just your iPhone and your TV on the same Wi-Fi.

  • Connects in under a second
  • Remembers your Fire TV — no re-setup on relaunch
  • Dark OLED UI — easy on the eyes at night
  • Open source — inspect every line
🔐

Secure & Private

Authentication uses RSA-2048 signing — the same protocol as the official ADB toolchain. Your private key is stored in the iOS Keychain. Nothing leaves your local network.

  • First-launch "Allow" prompt on Fire TV
  • Subsequent launches skip the prompt automatically
  • No internet access required after setup
Setup

Ready in four steps

One-time setup on the Fire TV. After that, the app connects instantly every time.

1

Enable Developer Options

On your Fire TV: Settings → My Fire TV → About → tap Build seven times until you see "You are now a developer."

2

Turn on ADB Debugging

Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options and set ADB Debugging: ON.

3

Find your Fire TV's IP

Settings → My Fire TV → About → Network — note the IP address (e.g. 192.168.1.45).

4

Connect in FireControl

Open the app, enter the IP, tap Connect. The first time, your Fire TV will ask to Allow — select "Always allow from this device". Done.

Fire TV — Developer Options
ADB Debugging ON
Apps from Unknown Sources Optional
Network IP 192.168.1.45
ADB Port 5555
FireControl Status Always Allowed

iPhone and Fire TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network

Requirements

What you need

📱

iPhone

iOS 17 or later. Side-loaded via Xcode or AltStore — not on the App Store yet.

📺

Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K

Any Fire TV device that supports ADB networking (most do). Stick 4K and Stick 4K Max confirmed.

📡

Same Wi-Fi Network

Your iPhone and Fire TV must be connected to the same local network. No internet required after setup.

🔊

Bluetooth Speaker (optional)

Any Bluetooth A2DP speaker paired to your iPhone — JBL Clip 4, Sony, Bose, etc. The volume slider appears automatically.

Open source & free, forever.

Clone it, build it in Xcode, run it on your phone. No App Store, no account, no catch.
